About Small Biz Blueprints

Plan smarter, avoid expensive mistakes, and move with confidence.

Our Purpose

Small Biz Blueprint was created to make starting and growing a small business more accessible and less overwhelming. Our goal is to provide practical tools, clear guidance, and trusted resources that help entrepreneurs move from ideas to action.

This website is built on a shared commitment to empowering small business owners with knowledge they can use. It was created through a collaboration between Christina Rhone, a local librarian with a background in academic, school, and public libraries, and Phydesia Lewis, Director of the Small Business Center at Alamance Community College, who brings over 30 years of experience in business consulting and workforce development.

The site was developed by Christopher Rhone, CEO of Arctic Raven Games.

What this tool does

The Business Reality Checker helps you turn a business idea into a real plan by walking you through the questions that actually matter like costs, time, readiness, and next steps.

  • Helps you organize your business idea into a clear plan
  • Pushes you to think through money, resources, and risks
  • Gives you a summary you can use to take action

1) Get your finances straight

Use the budget worksheets to understand what you can realistically afford.

2) Answer the planning questions

One step at a time, try noy to skip. So your plan isn’t missing critical parts.

3) Review your summary

Get a clear snapshot of what’s strong, what’s missing, and what you should do next.

Who it’s for

This is built for people starting small businesses, side hustles, creators, and anyone who wants a structured way to check if their plan makes sense before spending money.
